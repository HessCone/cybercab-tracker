      Data & method

      Source

      The Texas Department of Motor Vehicles publishes the vehicles each company has registered for automated (driverless) operation. This site reads Tesla's public roster from the TxDMV Motor Carrier Credentialing System (raw API).

      Counting

      Every vehicle is listed by VIN. VINs starting with 5YJAJEEU are counted as Cybercab, 7SAYGDEE as Model Y. A script checks the roster every hour and saves the count, so new VINs show up with the time they were first seen.

      Caveats

      Registration isn't the same as cars giving rides. Texas is the only state with a public Cybercab roster right now. Counts before tracking began come from public posts and are marked as such.
